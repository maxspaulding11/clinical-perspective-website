Who's Accepting Doctoral Students — Fall 2027

Clinical psychology runs on the mentor model — if your person isn't taking a student, the application usually can't go anywhere. This tracks who's accepting for Fall 2027, alongside what each program actually asks for: the application deadline, whether the GRE is required, how many letters of recommendation you'll need, and anything else worth knowing before you apply.

479 faculty confirmed accepting across 90 programs · last updated 2026-09-25

Every name here was read from the program's own page on the date shown, and the sentence it came from is quoted so you can judge it yourself. We never take faculty names from search results — when we tested that, search under-reported UCLA as 3 faculty when the real page listed 7, and FIU as 6 when the page listed 11.

“Cohort admission” marks programs that admit to the incoming class and assign an advisor afterwards, rather than admitting into a named faculty member’s lab — so there is no accepting-faculty list to post. Three programs say this outright on their own pages. For the rest, mostly practitioner PsyDs, it is our inference from the degree type, because their pages do not state a model either way. Treat it as a steer, not a fact about that program, and confirm before you apply.

This directory is being built and is nowhere near complete. There are roughly 250 APA-accredited clinical doctoral programs; only the ones below have been checked. A program not listed here means we haven't got to it — not that nobody is accepting. Most programs post their lists between August and October, so check back. Always confirm on the program's own page before you apply.
