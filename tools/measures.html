Find a Measure by Diagnosis

A quick-reference directory of validated clinical assessment instruments — search by diagnosis, construct, or instrument name and get what it measures, who it's normed for, whether it's free to use, and a ready-to-cite reference. Built for researchers, not just applicants — clinicians, faculty, and study teams alike.

Every reference is drawn from the instrument's original validation publication or its official publisher/source page — never reconstructed from memory. Access status is noted so you know whether it's free to use before you build a study design around it.

Need a reference for something else — an article, book, or report? The APA Citation Generator handles that: paste a DOI, PubMed ID or ISBN and get a formatted reference plus a Word reference page.

This is a growing directory. It doesn't cover every validated instrument yet, and access/licensing details can change — always confirm against the source before committing to a measure.
