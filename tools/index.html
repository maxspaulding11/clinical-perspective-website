Tools

Free tools for psychology students and researchers

Practical things we wished existed when we were applying, studying, and writing. All free, all in your browser — no accounts, no uploads, nothing sold on.

For applicants

Who's Accepting Doctoral Students

A directory of APA-accredited clinical psychology programs with a direct link to each one's own “faculty accepting students” page, plus named faculty where we've confirmed them at the source.

Browse programs →

Find a Professor by Research Interest

Type a topic — trauma, eating disorders, CBT, anything — and find clinical psychology PhD faculty who actually study it, with interests read from their own page.

Search professors →

Applying to Clinical Psychology PhD Programs

An honest walkthrough of how doctoral admissions in clinical psychology actually work — the mentor model, what the timeline looks like, and where applicants lose themselves points.

Read the guide →

For researchers

APA Citation Generator

Paste a DOI, PubMed ID or ISBN and get a correct APA 7 reference, both in-text forms, and a finished Word reference page — properly double-spaced with hanging indents.

Open the tool →

Find a Measure by Diagnosis

A searchable directory of 261 validated assessment instruments — the GAD-7, PHQ-9, PCL-5 and more — with free-use status and a ready-to-cite reference for each.

Search measures →
