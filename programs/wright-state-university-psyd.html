Wright State University

Clinical Psychology PsyD

Admits by cohort — no mentor list

Accepting students for Fall 2027?

There is no list to publish. This program admits to a cohort rather than to an individual advisor, so applicants are not matched to a single faculty member at the point of application. Checked September 23, 2026.

Applying

Deadline: November 16, 2026 (Fall 2027 admission)GRE: optional3 referencesFee: $50 SOPP processing fee, plus PsychologyCAS fee ($80 first program / $60 each additional)
  • Minimum GPA 3.0
  • Prerequisite coursework: intro psych, intro statistics, biopsychology, abnormal psych, research methods
  • Personal statement required (~500 words)
  • Mission/vision reflection required (~500 words)
  • CV/resume required
  • Official transcripts from all institutions
  • Interview required (letters recommended from doctoral-level professionals)
