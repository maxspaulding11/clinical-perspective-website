Rutgers - The State University of New Jersey

Clinical Psychology PsyD

Admits by cohort — no mentor list

Accepting students for Fall 2027?

There is no list to publish. This program admits to a cohort rather than to an individual advisor, so applicants are not matched to a single faculty member at the point of application. Checked September 23, 2026.

Applying

Deadline: December 1, 2026 (Fall 2027 entry)GRE: required3 referencesFee: $70
  • GRE Psychology Subject Test required (PsyD program only)
  • CV/resume required
  • Personal statement (1.5-2 pages) required
  • Supplementary essay (300-500 words) required
  • Prerequisite coursework: intro psych, stats, abnormal psych, biological bases, plus 1-2 electives
  • TOEFL required for foreign applicants (may be waived)
  • Restricted to one New Brunswick doctoral psych application per term
