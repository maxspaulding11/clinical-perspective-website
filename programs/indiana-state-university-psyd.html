Indiana State University

Clinical Psychology PsyD

Admits by cohort — no mentor list

Accepting students for Fall 2027?

There is no list to publish. This program admits to a cohort rather than to an individual advisor, so applicants are not matched to a single faculty member at the point of application. Checked August 31, 2026.

Applying

Deadline: December 1, 2026 (Fall cohort)GRE: not stated3 referencesFee: $45 (US/permanent residents); $65 (international)
  • Personal statement required
  • Current CV required
  • Minimum GPA 3.0 undergraduate
  • Prerequisite coursework: abnormal psych, research methods, statistics
  • Interview by invitation (~40% of applicants)
  • TOEFL required for international students
