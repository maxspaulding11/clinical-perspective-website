Do psychedelics affect memory and cognition?

By Maxamus Spaulding, BA

Updated September 11, 2026 · kept current as we cover new studies

The short answer

On the evidence we have covered: a single dose of psilocybin or MDMA showed no sign of impairing working memory in healthy volunteers. Memory task accuracy was actually higher at a three-month follow-up, and EEG recordings showed lasting changes in memory-related brain activity.

The caveat is large enough that the study's own authors lead with it: there was no control group. An improvement three months after doing a demanding memory task several times cannot be separated from simply having practised it. That is why this counts as an exploratory result rather than a finding.

It is also one study, at one dose, in healthy volunteers. It says nothing about repeated use, recreational doses, other drugs, or people with psychiatric conditions.

What this page covers. Cognition after single supervised doses in healthy volunteers, plus the trials we have covered on what psychedelics are being tested to treat. Not a safety review, and not evidence about recreational or repeated use. Every claim above is drawn from the studies below, and each of those links to the original paper.

  • Can one day of an inhaled psychedelic lift treatment-resistant depression?

    JAMA Psychiatry · June 2026

    In a phase 2b trial across 16 European sites, 81 adults with treatment-resistant depression received up to three escalating inhaled doses of mebufotenin (GH001) or placebo on a single day. One week later, 57.5% of the drug group (23 of 40) were in remission, compared with 0% of the 41 placebo patients.

Common questions

Do psychedelics damage memory?

The study we covered found no evidence of working-memory impairment from a single dose of psilocybin or MDMA in healthy volunteers. Because it had no control group, it cannot rule impairment in or out with confidence, and it does not speak to repeated use.

Why did memory scores improve?

Nobody knows from this study. Participants completed the same demanding memory task repeatedly across three months, and with no control group, practice is a straightforward explanation that the design cannot exclude.

How many people were studied?

29 people analysed per drug, with 16 taking both after a washout period, all healthy volunteers receiving a single dose.
